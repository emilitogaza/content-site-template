# Another Example

This page exists only to show a second entry under the **Examples** section in
the sidebar, and to give the Previous/Next pager somewhere to go.

## Why two example pages

Sections are just a shared `section` value across files. Because this file and
the [Markdown Showcase](/markdown-showcase) both set `section: Examples`, they're
grouped together, ordered by their `order` field.

## Replace me

When you build your real site, delete the example pages in `content/` and write
your own. Keep `content/README.md` around as a reference — it's ignored by the
site itself.

That's the end of the demo. Head back to [Welcome](/welcome) to start over, or
open `docs/theming.md` to re-skin the site.
